KNOWN LIMITATIONS - v0.1.3

Eyeballs retain L_Eye/R_Eye controls; eyelids do not blink. FACIAL_* influences
are collapsed to Head_0. Original RE8/RE9 faces contain blendshape sections;
the pinned reader disables their import as broken. No supported morph export
or facial control adaptation has been established for this release.

Dress movement uses skeletal weights, not cloth simulation. Rigid folds,
hem lifting and high edge strain occur in stronger synthetic crouch/stride
poses. Seam continuity does not prove body/cloth or self-collision avoidance.
The accepted thigh clearance and waist/sleeve weights remain unchanged.

Hair follows the corrected hood/head/neck/upper-body weighting. Native hair
secondary motion/collision is absent. Alpha-blind BVH overlap counts are only
diagnostics. User acceptance covers tested poses, not all cameras/cutscenes.

Grace boots are retained. Bela's original heels, decorative stitches and
animated cloak are not part of the established prototype scope. Separate
sleeve/glove construction and covered body cutouts are intentional.

Hair Strands must be OFF. Alternate outfits, damage/limb variants, dedicated
cutscene resources and camera-specific first-person routing are not covered
by the base-part package. Materials approximate some original facial effects;
wrinkle masks, blood/wetness and eye overlays need separate research.

Grace's playable animations and controller remain active. No original Bela
body/facial animation conversion or voice replacement is included.

v0.1.3 has current offline validation. No new v0.1.3 runtime test is claimed.
Walking/running, crouch transitions, reloads, interactions, foot IK and wider
cutscene compatibility require explicitly recorded in-game confirmation.
